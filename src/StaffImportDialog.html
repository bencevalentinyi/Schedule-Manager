Import Staff Data

Fills in staff-sheet columns (Employee ID, Email, Role, your own columns…) from a file, a sheet tab or another Google Sheet. Nothing is written until you click Apply.

    Choose the source

    Where is the data?

    A Quinyx "Custom report" (Badge no. + Name) is recognised: the name finds each person and the badge becomes their Employee ID.

    Choose the columns

    Find each person by

    matches

    A person is found when this value equals their staff-sheet value (names: "Last, First" is tried too; capitals and extra spaces don't matter).

    Fill in

    Empty cells in the source never clear anything.

    Teams none ticked = every team
