Schedule Manager imports Quinyx schedule exports into Google Sheets, stores every shift and meeting once in _SCHEDULE_DATA, and renders a daily view and a staff sheet per team.

Typical workflow

  1. Setup & Configuration → Setup / Configure Spreadsheet creates the internal sheets (run once; safe to repeat).
  2. Create New Team: name, operating hours, staff columns and shift types. It creates the team's daily sheet and staff sheet.
  3. Fill the staff directory (Schedule Management → View Staff Directory). Employee ID is the matching key.
  4. Schedule Import → Import Quinyx Export: choose the team and the file, confirm the date, choose the shift types, review the preview, apply.
  5. Schedule Management → Schedule Viewer: move between dates, filter, and refresh. You can also change the date in cell B3 or the filters in row 3 of a daily sheet.

Key rules

  • Nothing is written until you confirm the preview. Every import can be undone (Undo Last Import, newest first).
  • Duplicates are detected with the Quinyx shift ID when mapped, otherwise with team + employee + start + end + activity (+ shift type).
  • "Replace" only affects the imported team(s) and date(s), and marks shifts REMOVED instead of deleting them.
  • Meetings are separate assignments. A meeting inside a shift is not a conflict and does not add working hours.
  • Only the shift types selected for a team are imported. A team without a selection must choose before its first import.
  • Operating hours (standard, 24/7, or custom across midnight) control which shifts the daily view includes and the headcount range.
  • Daily sheets are views: edits there are overwritten on refresh. Change data by importing.
  • Internal "_" sheets are protected with a warning only. Hiding or protecting sheets is not security.

Troubleshooting

  • A sheet is missing or renamed: Maintenance → Rebuild Missing Sheets, then Validate Spreadsheet Structure.
  • Formatting looks wrong, or meetings are not shaded: Maintenance → Repair Formatting.
  • XLSX upload error: enable the Drive advanced service (Apps Script editor → Services → Drive API), or export CSV.
  • Dialog buttons do nothing: you are probably signed into several Google accounts. Use a window signed into only this account.
  • Technical errors are logged in the Apps Script editor under Executions.
